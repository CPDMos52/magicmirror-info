Privacy Policy

MagicMirror Family OS is a private household dashboard operated for personal family use.

Google Calendar

The app uses read-only Google Calendar access to display household events. Calendar data is used only for this private dashboard and is not sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Storage and security

Calendar credentials and calendar data are stored on the household’s private server. The public website does not collect calendar data, passwords, or personal information.

Contact

For questions about this app, contact the app owner through the Google account associated with the project.
